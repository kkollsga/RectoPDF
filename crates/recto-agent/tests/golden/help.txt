recto: control RectoPDF from the command line.

Start with `recto status`. `recto help <command>` explains one. Every command takes `--json`. Flags take `--flag value` or `--flag=value`. `recto --version` gives the version.

Start here:
  status               Whether the session is running, and what access agents have.
  help                 This list, or everything about one command.
  version              This program's version, and the API version it speaks.

Read a document:
  tabs                 The open tabs, with each one's page, length and unsaved edits.
  where                Where one tab is: its page, its length, and whether it has unsaved edits.
  facts                A document's facts: pages, size, version, and whether it has contents.
  contents             A document's table of contents, a piece at a time.
  search               Finds words across a document.
  index                Asks for the tab's search index to be built, in the background, if it isn't there, and says where it stands.
  index status         Where the tab's search index stands.
  index clear          Throws away the tab's search index and generated contents, or with `--all` every document's.
  text                 One page's text, normalised for reading and search, with a flag for text that looks hidden.
  select               What a click, a drag or a stretch of characters takes on a page: the text, in reading order, and where it shows.
  copy                 The text ⌘C would copy for the same pick, as far as one copy goes: 16 MiB, 100,000 pages or 2 minutes.
  render               Draws a page to a new PNG file, sharp or not at all.
  export svg           Writes a page, or a box on it, to a new SVG file, whole or not at all.
  images               Where a page's images are, numbered in the order the page draws them, with each one's own size and whether it's a JPEG.
  image                One of a page's images to a new file, as itself, whole or not at all.
  snapshot             A box on a page, as the snapshot tool takes it: a PNG or JPG drawn sharp at the scale asked for, an SVG cut to the box as `export svg --rect` writes it, or its text, by a copy's rules.
  print                Prints pages to a new PDF, through the same drawing the app's File > Print uses: the real pages, drawn at print resolution, never the screen's, with no paper colour, and unsaved turns as the edits have them.
  signatures           What the file says about signatures.
  sidebar              The sidebar's rows, as the app would draw them.
  links                A page's links and where each goes: a page of this document, a web or email address, or refused, as a script, a program or a file on the Mac always is.

Open and move around:
  open                 Opens a PDF as the active tab, or goes to its tab if it's already open.
  goto                 Goes to a page.
  goto label           Goes to the page the file calls this, such as `iv` or `A-3`, as the go-to field reads it: a label first, then a plain number no page is called.
  back                 Goes back to where the tab was before its last jump, as ⌘[ does: a link, a contents entry or `goto`.
  forward              Goes forward again after `back`, as ⌘] does.
  follow               Follows a link within the document, as a click does, so `recto back` comes back.
  recent               Files opened lately, newest first, as File > Open Recent lists them, with the tab each is open in and its whole path, which `recto open` can open.
  recent clear         Empties the list of files opened lately, as Open Recent > Clear Menu does.
  close                Closes a tab.
  tap                  Taps a tab in the sidebar: switches to it, or folds its contents if it's already active.
  compare              Tabs side by side, as ⌘-clicking them in the sidebar does: each its own document in its own place, zoom always the same.
  contents fold        Folds a section of the contents the sidebar shows, or opens it if it's folded, as the chevron beside it does.
  contents show        Which contents the sidebar shows for a tab, as the note over them does: the file's own, or generated ones, made from the headings on its pages if they aren't yet.
  contents stop        Stops generated contents being made for a tab and shows the file's own, as the note's Stop does.
  view                 How the app shows pages: the paper, the sidebar, the agent icon, the tab showing's layout and the zoom.
  palette              ⌘K's command palette in the app: what a few letters find among its commands and open tabs, best first, worded and ranked as the palette shows them.
  find                 The app's Find, in the document being read (in a comparison, the pane being read): what the Find field in the sidebar does.

Change pages, then save:
  page rotate          Turns pages clockwise.
  page move            Moves pages, so the first of them ends up at `--to`.
  page delete          Removes pages from the edited document.
  page insert          Adds every page of another PDF.
  edits                A tab's unsaved edits, as runs of pages.
  discard              Throws away a tab's edits, undone ones included.
  undo                 Reverses the last page edit.
  redo                 Repeats the last undone page edit.
  clear-history        Clears a tab's edit history: every step undo or redo could take, and its file on disk.
  save                 Saves a tab's edits into its file.
  save-as              Saves a tab's document as a new file, and the tab goes over to it.
  edit mode            Turns the window's edit mode on or off, as the user does: on, the sidebar shows its tools for editing pages and its Rotate turns the page being read rather than every page.

Wait instead of polling:
  wait tab-open        Blocks until a tab matching the selector is open.
  wait tab-closed      Blocks until no tab matches the selector.
  wait page-drawn      Blocks until a page has been drawn (by `render`, or by `text`'s check), or failed to be.
  wait contents-generated Waits for the tab's generated contents to be made, asking for them if nobody has.
  wait indexing-done   Waits for the tab's search index to be finished, asking for it to be built if nobody has.
  watch                Prints events from now on, one per line.
  watch stop           Ends every watch running on this session, each with its last line as usual.

Access and history:
  permission show      What access agents have now, and what each level allows.
  permission request   Asks the user for more access.
  log                  What agents have done: time, command, level and outcome, oldest first.
  sign                 Signing isn't open to agents at any level.
  permission set       For the user: sets what agents may do.

The session:
  serve                Runs the session, on a socket only this user can reach, with no network listener.
  stop                 Ends the session.
  local                For tests: runs one command in a throwaway session in this process, with one file open.
  session              For QA scripts: a throwaway session in this process, with one JSON command per line on stdin and one reply per line.
  session show         What the state record holds, and what's become of each tab's file: moved, changed, missing or needing its password again.
  session sleep        Puts a tab to sleep now, as five minutes left alone would: its sleep file's written, its journal's compacted, and its contents, edits, worker and tiles leave memory.
  session save         Writes the state record now and reads it back, returning an error if it doesn't match.
  session restore      Puts the session back as the state record has it, with every tab cold but the active one.
  session check        Whether this is a safe moment to hand off to a new version: no sheet open, no command or file read under way.

The app itself:
  update status        What version is running, what's on offer, how things stand, and how the last update went.
  update check         Looks for a new version now and waits for the answer, by what Check for Updates… does.
  update install       Installs what's on offer, by what Update to X.Y.Z… does: downloads and verifies with the app in use, then waits for anything in flight, hands off and reopens.
  update dev-builds    Allow development builds, as Settings has it: off unless the owner's turned it on.
  settings             The app's settings, as the Settings window shows them.

Skill files:
  skill show           Prints the generated skill.
  skill install        For the user: writes SKILL.md and REFERENCE.md into their agent's files, after a word typed back at a real terminal.

Measuring:
  budget               The session's own footprint, tabs and handles right now.
  budget render        Measures a worker on a tab's document: idle, pages, peak and retirement.
  budget viewport      Measures a reader's window: first view, one scroll step, one zoom step.
  budget tabs          Opens one PDF as many tabs, through links in a temp folder, and reports memory and handles.
  budget index         Builds one PDF's index in the system's temp folder and reports what that costs, and search times with and without it.

Testing the app:
  qa start             Starts the app in a quiet run on this state folder, which must be set with RECTO_STATE_DIR: off every screen, with no Dock icon and never active.
  qa run               Runs a QA script against the running app, and prints PASS or FAIL for it and each line that fails.
  qa budget            Measures the app against the budgets on a release build, starting and ending its own quiet runs: cold start, one document, scrolling, idle, a closed window, many tabs.
  qa compare-budget    Measures comparison view against the budgets, starting and ending its own quiet runs: memory at rest and after scrolling, idle wake-ups, the UI thread's step in a sweep, and starts from the record with the comparison in it.
  qa select-budget     Measures selecting on one file, starting and ending its own quiet run: a first double-click, a long drag over several pages, select all, and a copy of all of it, with the footprint before, during and after, and the UI thread's worst step.
  qa state             What the app is showing, as it says itself: the window, the sidebar, the page, the tiles, memory and stalls.
  qa shot              Writes a PNG of the window's content, drawn inside the app at 2x.
  qa wait-settled      Blocks until everything visible is drawn and on its layers, or marked as it is, and nothing in the window is still moving: the sidebar contents' fade and slide, their drift to the place, a name about to show in full, a zoom about to go sharp.
  qa appearance        Light or dark, for this app alone.
  qa scroll            Scrolls by this many points, by what a scroll does.
  qa zoom              Sets the zoom, by what the end of a pinch does.
  qa hover             Shows a sidebar row as hovered, by what the pointer entering it does, with no pointer.
  qa pane-scroll       Scrolls one pane of a comparison by what a scroll calls: the reader's own scroll, so it's the pane read and synced scroll follows it.
  qa pane-context-menu Right-clicks a pane of a comparison, by what AppKit calls for a right-click, and chooses an item, as a person would: the pane is the one read, and the item acts on it.
  qa pane-focus        Makes a pane of a comparison the one read, by what AppKit calls for a right-click on it, with the menu made and not shown.
  qa compare-click     ⌘-clicks a sidebar row, by what that click calls, with no pointer or key: a tab goes side by side with what's showing, or leaves.
  qa click-row         Clicks a sidebar row, by what a click calls, with no pointer.
  qa contents-scroll   Scrolls the sidebar's contents so a row is showing, as the reader's own scroll does, with no pointer.
  qa contents-pointer  Says the pointer has come into the sidebar's contents or left them, as the real one does, with no pointer.
  qa corner-pointer    Says the pointer has come over a corner's mark or left it, as the corner's tracking area does, with no pointer: `top-left` the window's buttons and the sidebar's, `top-right` the zoom level, `lower-right` the page number.
  qa reduce-motion     Acts as though the system's Reduce Motion setting were on or off, for this app alone.
  qa accessibility     Does what the system does to the app's own views for VoiceOver and its display settings, by the same methods, with nothing posted: Increase Contrast on or off for this app alone, a scroll thumb's increment or decrement, a contents row's fold action, or an arrow key with the sidebar's list holding the keyboard.
  qa reveal            Shows what hovering over a sidebar row reveals, by what the pointer resting there calls, with no pointer.
  qa resize            Resizes the window, in points.
  qa window            Closes the window or opens it again, by what the red button and the Dock icon do.
  qa entry-points      Checks what AppKit reaches the app by, which the other verbs go round: that each owner answers the selector AppKit sends, and for those that can be, that going by it works.
  qa scroller-style    Uses classic or overlay scrollers, as a Mac with a mouse connected or without one does.
  qa menu              Chooses an item of the menu bar, through the menu item itself.
  qa button            Clicks one of the window's own buttons, through the button itself: `sidebar` is the one beside the window's controls that shows and hides the sidebar, and `zoom` the zoom level beside it.
  qa zoom-type         Types a zoom level into the field the zoom level opens, and presses Return, through the field's own action.
  qa context-menu      Chooses an item of the page's right-click menu, through the menu item itself: the layouts, or Copy and Select All.
  qa key-action        Does Undo or Redo as its key or menu item does with the window key: given to the first in the window's own chain that answers it, if that says it can be chosen.
  qa password          Types a password into the sheet a protected PDF asks with, and presses Open, through the sheet's own ending.
  qa page-type         Types into the go-to field the page number opens, and presses Return, through the field's own action: a page number, or what the document calls a page, such as `iv`.
  qa page-escape       Presses Escape in the go-to field, by the method AppKit calls for it: the field closes and the page stays as it was.
  qa swipe             Swipes on the page, by what the system's swipe calls, with its sideways amount: as in Safari, a positive one goes back and a negative one forward.
  qa zoom-escape       Presses Escape in the zoom level's field, by the method AppKit calls for it: the field closes and the zoom stays as it was.
  qa panel             Clicks the sidebar's panel button, or one of the panel's own, through the button itself.
  qa mouse-down        Presses the mouse on a page, through what a real press calls, from the window's coordinates, at whatever the zoom is.
  qa mouse-drag        Drags to a place, by what a real drag calls.
  qa print-to-pdf      Prints the tab showing to a PDF through File > Print's own path, the pages Rust prepares handed to the system's printing, with no panel shown: on paper the first page's size, with no margins, so it lays page for page over the source.
  qa mouse-up          Lets the mouse go, by what a real release calls.
  qa pointer           Moves the pointer to a place, by what a real move calls.
  qa copied            What the app's last copy put on the clipboard: its text, and every type on it in order, with each one's size and SHA-256.
  qa paste             Asks for one type from the clipboard as another app's paste would, so a type the app only promised, as a snapshot's SVG, is made now.
  qa services          Asks what the Services menu asks: whether the app has text to send, and writes it to a clipboard of this run's own.
  qa notice-button     Clicks the button on the notice by the page, through the button itself: "Copy anyway", "Print anyway" or a print's "Stop", whatever Rust gave it.
  qa stall             Blocks the app's UI thread on purpose, to show the hang watchdog works.
  qa sweep             Scrolls steadily at a speed for a time, by what a scroll does, and measures the UI thread and memory meanwhile.
  qa full-screen       Says the window went into full screen or came out of it, by what AppKit tells the app.
  qa focus             Gives the keyboard to the page, the sidebar or Find, by what a click does.
  qa frontmost         Says the app is the active one or not, by what AppKit tells it.
  qa minimise          Says the window went into the Dock or came out of it, by what AppKit tells the app.
  qa hide              Says the app was hidden (⌘H) or shown again, by what AppKit tells it.
  qa sidebar-width     Sets the sidebar's width, by what dragging its edge does.
  qa find              Opens or closes Find in the sidebar, by what ⌘F and Escape do.
  qa find-query        Sets what Find has found for, leaving what's in the field as it is, as a record from before a restart does.
  qa find-draft        Sets what's in Find's field without finding it, as text typed and not run that a record brings back.
  qa find-type         Types into Find's field, by what a change in the field calls: it's found at once, the last query stopping.
  qa find-key          Presses a key in Find's field, by what the field calls for it: Return goes to the next hit, ⇧Return to the one before, Escape closes Find.
  qa find-click        Clicks a row of Find's list, by what a click calls: a hit's row goes to it and selects it on the page.
  qa palette-type      Types into ⌘K's palette, by what a change in its field calls.
  qa palette-key       Presses a key in ⌘K's palette, by what its field calls for it: ↑ and ↓ move the selection, Return runs it, Escape closes the palette.
  qa edit-mode         Goes into edit mode or out.
  qa view-rotation     Turns the active tab's view by quarter turns, as the state record carries it: every page is drawn turned on screen, and nothing about the file changes.
  qa sheet-answer      Answers the sheet over the window, as its button does: the close prompt's `first` is Save, `second` Cancel and `third` Don't Save; a save problem's `first` is OK and `second` Save As; a link's `first` is Open, which a quiet run notes in `app.links.address_opened` and opens nothing, and `second` Cancel; `cancel` closes Save As's sheet.
  qa save-as           What Save As's sheet does when it's answered with a new file's path: the window's save, off its thread, the tab going over to the new file.
  qa save-from         A quiet run's test of what happens around a save's writing, with a file the caller has written in place of the save's own writer: keeps the pages undo needs, notes what's coming, puts the file in place of the active tab's, and confirms.
  qa owner-edit        Makes an edit as the owner, as the app's own tools will: `rotate:PAGE`, `delete:PAGE`, `undo` or `redo`.
  qa note-input        Says the user just touched the app, by what the event monitor calls.
  qa open-file         Opens a file by the route a drop or the menu takes, which reads it off the session thread.
  qa hold-reads        Holds files being read for opening where they are until it's told otherwise, as a slow disk would, for what's waiting on them to be seen.
  qa hand-off-quit     Asks to quit for an update, by what the updater calls.
  qa sheet             Says a sheet or panel opened or closed over the window, by what opening one does.
  qa update-tick       Does what the update timer does when it goes off, by the same call, with no waiting for it.
  qa update-clock      Moves the updater's clock on, in a test run with its own update server and nowhere else, so the once-a-day and 30-minute rules can be tested with no waiting.
  qa settings          Opens or closes the Settings window, off every screen, or clicks one of its switches, or its privacy link, through the control itself.
  qa shot-settings     Writes a PNG of the Settings window, drawn inside the app at 2x.
  qa shot-menus        Writes a PNG of every menu of the menu bar side by side, each followed by any submenu of its items, such as File > Open Recent, drawn as `qa shot-menu` draws one.
  qa shot-menu         Writes a PNG of a menu's real items, drawn inside the app at 2x in the menu's place.
  qa quit              Quits the app, by what the Quit menu item does.
  qa handoff           Hands off as an update does: flushes, reads back, and quits with no prompt.
  qa dormant-after     How many seconds left alone makes a tab go dormant, in place of five minutes.
  qa hold-drawing      Holds all drawing until it's told otherwise, so a tab's first frame stays to be looked at: `on` or `off`.
  qa sleep-write-gap   How many seconds a tab that's only moving about waits for its next sleep file, in place of five minutes.
  qa live-budget       How many MiB the documents off screen may hold, in place of 256.
  qa memory-pressure   What the system saying memory's short calls: `warning` or `critical`.

Exit codes: 0 ok, 1 failed, 2 usage, 3 not found, 4 ambiguous, 5 permission denied, 6 not running, 7 timed out, 8 protected, 10 busy.
