- Other programs: "It should be as cross platform compatible as possible without limo our feature options, ie adobe destination links": a page (`manual.pdf#page=42`), a named destination inside the PDF (`manual.pdf#nameddest=FaultModeling`), a spot on a page (`manual.pdf#page=42&zoom=100,0,350`). - How to go about it: "Basically think this through plan it out. Break up the elephant into pieces and place the pieces into our plan where it makes sense".

## Product rules from the owner: a zoomed page stays centred (2026-11-16)
- Owner, 2026-11-16, evening, using development build 0.1.1.5: "when I zoom into the page only the right margin is reduced. Why? The page should always be centered (if there is no scrollbar)". Why it did so, as found when it was built (the coordinator's first account, a left margin the layout kept, was wrong): a zoom is the window's own magnification, and the layout was never told of it, so the document stayed as wide as the window at any zoom; a page zoomed but still narrower than the window could then be scrolled sideways off the middle, a zoom about the pointer left it off the middle, and a change of tab put it back at the left. A plain zoom about the middle was even (261 and 261 points at 150% in a window of 1,440). **The rule: while a row of pages is no wider than the window, it is centred in it, with equal margins left and right, and there is no sideways scrollbar.** The coordinator's readings: the margins kept at a fit (fit to width, a new document) are as they were; zoomed past them, the two margins shrink together to nothing; wider than the window, the row starts at the window's left edge and scrolls sideways; a zoomed page may pass under the agent icon and the zoom level in the corner, which replaces "nothing sits over the page" for a page the person has zoomed that wide; a comparison's panes follow the same rule in their own width.
