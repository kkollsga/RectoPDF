# The sidebar

> Part of the engineering notes: [`README.md`](README.md).

**Sections fold** (owner, 2026-11-07: "there should be a collapse toggle button for sections with sub chapters on the left side of the headers"). An entry with entries under it has a chevron just left of its name, with the pointer in the sidebar, as every detail is. What following the place opens is the default, and `Session::toggle_contents_section` keeps only what the reader set against it, at most 256 a tab, in the record as `contents_folds`, so a cold tab that was never folded pays a pointer. **A shortened name shows in full at once,** in the row's own colour (owner: "The hover delay before showing the full toc name makes in not intuitive, and the expanded name should have the color of the title"): `reveal_delay_seconds` is 0. **The thumb** (owner: "The sidebar thumb should always be visible, and lets hide the rail", "the thumb should be thinner", and for both scrollers "the scroll thumb always visible, the rail always hidden"): the sidebar's lists have no system scroller at all, and a `ThumbView` of their own over the right margin, 4 points wide at rest, 6 under the pointer or dragged, 3 from the edge, grabbed anywhere in a 12-point strip, shown only when there's something to scroll, the same whatever the system's setting and moving with the contents' following. Its widths and colours are `recto_session::SCROLL_THUMB`, which the app reads as `AppCore.scroll_thumb()`, for the page area to share. Why 4: the system's overlay thumb is about 7 at rest, and 4 is the thinnest that still reads as a thumb on a 1x screen. Names stop 14 points from the edge and page numbers 18, so it covers neither. AppKit's overlay scroller wasn't used for it: it fades by itself, and isn't drawn into a QA shot.

**The contents area.** `sidebar_heights` in `recto-session` shares the sidebar's height. The contents never take over `CONTENTS_MAX_SHARE`, 60% of it (owner, 2026-11-07: "The max height of toc does not seem to be working"), so a long outline with one tab leaves the rest empty. Other tabs and the footer stay, and a longer tab list scrolls by itself. Contents are one `NSTableView` pulling rows from Rust with `contents_rows(first, count)`, so a 10,000-entry outline holds about 29 row views.

**Showing a shortened name.** One tracking area on the sidebar. After a short rest over a cut-off row, a label opens in place over it, past the sidebar's edge if it must, and goes on leaving. Rows don't move. A timer exists only during that short wait. Rust gives the text (`row_full_text`): cleaned of control and direction characters and held to 400. `recto qa hover` and `qa reveal` report what it would show.

**Which version of the contents the host has.** A chapter closing is a change to the list, sent as where it differs from the one before, with a version. The diff stays with its version until the next change. The host applies version + 1, nothing for the same version, and reloads if it's further behind, so a publish twice for one change or two changes in one pull can't leave it wrong.

**Many tabs.** The tabs' rows cross to the UI thread only when their version moves. The active tab's index comes with the model, so a table reload isn't quadratic. A tile drawn doesn't go through the sidebar at all.
