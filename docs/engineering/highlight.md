# Highlights

> Part of the engineering notes: [`README.md`](README.md).

**A highlight is an edit like a turn** (R4.3, the pilot of [`../FEATURES.md`](../FEATURES.md)). `recto highlight` (Control) highlights exactly what `recto select` takes for the same arguments, and Mark Up > Highlight (⌃⌘H) sends the person's selection as that command, run for them by the one executor. Its state is `recto_session::edit::marks`: steps of `PageEdits`' one history (`Step::Mark`), so undo, redo, the journal, the Save icon, discard, the close prompt and who made each step hold as for a turn, and the owner's-edits rules too. A highlight belongs to a page by what the page is, `(source, page)`, so it follows its page through moves and turns; across pages it's one step. Its rectangles are in the page's frame, as `select` gives them, before any turn an edit adds. `recto highlights` lists them, the file's own taken out, and with `--page` the file's own (`recto_core::highlights`: geometry, colour, place and `/Rect`, never a string, capped, time-limited and fuzzed).

**A file's own highlight is taken out as an edit too** (review of R4.3, S2). `recto highlight remove` (and Remove Highlight) takes the edits and the file's own highlights its point or pick touches as one step (`MarkStep::RemoveFile`, inverse `RestoreFile`), naming each by page, place in `/Annots` and `/Rect` (`FileMark`; the history keeps its lines too). Only the tab's own file's: one on a page added from another PDF is refused (`highlight_in_added_file`) until a save makes the page the file's. While unsaved it's hidden (`Marks::hidden`, keys only) and the next Save takes it out of the file by a rewrite, checked against the file first (a `/Highlight` at that place, `/Rect` within 0.5 pt, or the Save refuses), its popup with it, and gone however else the file reaches it (a second entry, another page's list, a reply's `/IRT`, the structure tree's `/OBJR`): the rewrite's `remove_object` drops every reference to it, and a reply stays as a note of its own. A page or the catalog dressed as a highlight or popup is never removed. A rewrite needs Full of an agent, and a signed file's Save is off (Save As makes the unsigned copy), as for a delete.

**The history goes on past a Save** (owner, 2026-11-07; S2 and S3). The save reports where each highlight went (`recto_core::save::MarksWritten`: its place in `/Annots` and `/Rect`; after a rewrite read back from the file written, as the rewrite takes more out of kept pages' lists), what it took out, and the size a page an edit turned was shown at before the turn; `marks::saved` carries every step on from it: a saved highlight's step takes the file's annotation out, a removal the save made puts the highlight back as a new edit, a file highlight still there is renamed by its new position and place, a turned page's rectangles turn with it, and a highlight on a page the save left out stays (on the kept copy) to come back with it. What can't be carried (a file highlight on a page left out, a turned page without its size, anything after a crash between writing and confirming) rolls off, counted; an agent's Save below Full that would roll off the owner's is refused (`owner_edits_at_stake`). A journal says version 2 whenever anything in it is a highlight's or a removal's.

**Drawing.** The app gives each pane's page view the tab's set when it changes (made once per change, by the list's tick), as one op (`Op::Marks`), and the host (`PageMarks.swift`) draws a layer of each page's. A saved highlight is part of the page PDFium draws. One taken out and unsaved is left out by the worker: the page view holds the set by (tab, file) while a shown tab has one (`Shared.hidden`), `renderer_for` hands it to the job's renderer, which sends `recto_proto::hidden::HiddenAnnots` ahead of the job when the worker holds another, and the worker ORs `FPDF_ANNOT_FLAG_HIDDEN` into each matching annotation's flags in PDFium's copy in memory as the page loads, never writing anything (a source scan holds that nothing in `recto-render` can). A set's change draws again the pages it's on, and what the tab had in flight isn't kept when it lands (its floor is raised); a tab is given its set before its pane shows it. An agent's render, snapshot, SVG and print of a tab, and the person's Snapshot tool, follow the same set (`Marks::hidden_annots`, made once), and a source scan holds every renderer opened outside tests to it.

**Costs.** A tab without highlights: nothing (no edit list, no op, no entry in the page view's map, nothing sent to a worker). A highlight: a 32-byte `Mark` and 16 bytes a line, boxed in its history step; at most 256 Ki lines a document. A file highlight taken out: 28 bytes in the session (`FileKey`), 28 on the wire and 12 in the worker once its page is loaded (page, place, flags), and its lines in the history's step (48 bytes and 16 a line, under the history's cap); at most 10,000 a document. The engine's shared state grew 64 bytes a pane (an empty map). A reconcile pass with 10,000 highlights: 3.9 ms before, 195 us a rebuild now, and nothing while they don't change.
