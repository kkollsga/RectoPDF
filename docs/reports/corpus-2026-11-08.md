# Corpus run, 2026-11-08

Every PDF of bench100 (`distillPDF/benchmarking/bench100`) through `recto`, on the release build of `build/release-reports` at `d4116a5` plus the notices commit (no reader code changed). Phase 7: "Corpus run across all benchmark PDFs, with a dated report".

**How.** `tools/corpus_run.py`, through `tools/sysguard/heavy.sh`, on this Mac (M4, 16 GB). Each step is its own `recto local FILE ...` process on a throwaway state folder, so every step pays a process start and a worker's start: facts, `render` of the first and the last page, `text` of the first and the last page, `search` for a word no page has (so every page is read, with no index), and `export svg` of page 1. Peak memory is the whole tree, `recto` and its workers, by `phys_footprint` polled every 5 ms: the largest sum at one poll, or the largest single process's own lifetime peak if that's higher. **Under load:** the queue had this run in one of 3 slots, the 24-hour soak was running beside it at background priority, and the load average was 4 to 6. Times are wall clock and include that load.

## Result

**141 files, 5,872 pages, 841 MiB: every step of every file succeeded.** No failure, no refusal, no timeout, no crash of a worker that wasn't recovered. No file is copy-restricted, so no export asked for `--anyway`. One file is encrypted with no user password, `un_report_un_sdg2023`, and opens, draws, reads and exports like the rest. Every render came out at 2x, none lowered to fit the memory limit.

## Worth knowing

- **The USGS map (`gov_usgs_usgs70277338`, 346 MiB, one page) is the peak of every step,** as before: render 431 MiB by the tree (the worker 396), text of its page 488 MiB, because the hidden-text check draws the page at 2x, and SVG 735 MiB in 4.3 s, in the larger worker after the first gave up at its 512 MiB limit (727 MiB in that worker, 7 MiB in `recto`). That matches `docs/ENGINEERING.md`'s 730 MiB. All of it is under the worker limits and the named exception for huge images; `recto` itself never passed 35 MiB on any step of any file.
- **Next heaviest:** NASA `ntrs20220005969`, the page of shaded backgrounds that misses the colour-page budget, at 220 MiB for a render and 314 MiB for its first page's SVG; then USGS `70277269` and `70277797` at 137 to 168 MiB. Everything else stays under 110 MiB, p95 57 to 76 MiB.
- **Workers started again on the way:** a search of NASA `ntrs20240002628` used 8 workers in a row (1.6 s for 383 pages), as heavy picture pages retire a worker past its line and the next page gets a fresh one, which is the design (`docs/ENGINEERING.md`, "The search index"). 12 other steps used 2: the map's SVG, as above, and 11 text reads and searches on picture-heavy pages, where a page past the first worker's line finished in a fresh one.
- **90 pages have no text at all,** in 14 files (NASA, USGS, one World Bank report and one arXiv paper): scans and full-page pictures. Search reports them as `pages_with_no_text`; `text` gives 0 characters and no error. Two files have no text on page 1.
- **Hidden text flagged on page 1** of 15 files, the World Bank cover pages, NASA `ntrs20180005693`, and USGS `70277338` and `70277796`, as the survey in `docs/ENGINEERING.md` found.
- **SVG:** 51 first pages export all vector; the other 90 carry images, at their own resolution. The largest SVG is the map's, 56.4 MiB.

Nothing here is a bug, so there's nothing to fix or track from this run. A measuring mistake was found and fixed in the script itself: the first pass took `recto`'s peak from `wait4`, whose peak resident size on macOS takes in the reaped workers' too, and read 530 MiB for `recto` on the map's SVG where its own footprint is 7 MiB.

**Not covered:** the app's own window over these files (the soak opens all 141 in the app, see its report), pages past the first and last for render and text, files outside bench100 (the S-1, Moderna, `attention`), a password-protected file, and a cold file cache: every file had been read before.
