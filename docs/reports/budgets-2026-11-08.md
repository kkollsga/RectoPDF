# Budgets against SPEC §2, 2026-11-08

Every line of `SPEC.md` §2 measured on the release build (`app/build.sh --release`, fat LTO) of `build/release-reports` from `d4116a5`, whose reader code is `main`'s. Phase 7: "Final budget report against every line of `SPEC.md` §2".

**How, and under what load.** This Mac (M4, 16 GB). Quiet runs: the window is off every screen, so frames reaching a display, a visible window's backing store and 120 Hz aren't in any of this, and the memory figures are a floor until a run on a real screen. Every run went through the build queue, with 1 to 2 of its 3 slots busy (another agent's builds came and went) and the 24-hour soak running beside it at background priority, at load average 3.3 to 5.7. Nothing was measured alone (`HEAVY_SOLO` is the coordinator's). Whole process tree unless it says.

The runs:
- **A:** `recto qa budget --file rfc9110 --heavy NASA ntrs20240002628 --runs 11`.
- **B:** `recto qa budget --file <made 2 GiB, 10,000-page PDF> --heavy USGS 70277338 (the 346 MiB map) --runs 11`. The made file: a classic xref, each page a line of text and a 210 KiB stream beside it, 2,155,039,645 bytes.
- **B2:** 21 more starts on the made 2 GiB file. `qa budget` with `--runs 21` ran out of its quiet run's 10 minutes in the 1,000-tab stage, as each tab of a fresh clone pays the cold open below, and gives nothing when it stops; so a script did what its cold start does: the app started with the file, timed to `qa wait-settled`, and `qa state`'s worst stall for each.
- **Extra:** a quiet run on each colour page, at rest on it and 4 s later; and 5 runs of waking a sleeping tab (`rfc9110` p40 put to sleep with `session sleep`, NASA showing, then `tap`), read from `qa state`'s `last_wake`.
- **Tabs again:** 1,000 tabs of the map, three runs, as `qa budget` does it, after run B's single reading came out high.
- **Sweeps again:** six `qa sweep 6 --for 5s` in a row on `rfc9110` from page 64, after A and B each read a blank step on a seen page.

## What missed, and where it's tracked

- **Cold open of a 10,000-page file, about 2x over.** `IndexedReader::page_map` resolves every page object when a file is first opened, a scattered `pread` each, about 0.9 s of a 1.07 s open when they aren't cached. It's the same for each of 1,000 tabs of APFS clones of that file, as each clone has its own cache, so B2's `qa budget` took about 1.5 s a tab in its 1,000-tab stage and ran out of time. New, `docs/PLAN.md`.
- **One blank step on a seen page** in a 6-pages-a-second sweep that starts from rest, in two of six sweeps. The last report had 0. New, `docs/PLAN.md`, with the steps to see it.
- **Waking a sleeping tab: sharp at 100 to 238 ms** against 100 on a text page, in 3 of 5 runs. First frame and blank frames meet theirs. New, `docs/PLAN.md`.
- **The UI thread's worst stall, 20 to 27 ms** against 16. Known, and the coordinator's quiet-time comparison is already in `docs/PLAN.md`.
- **Shaded backgrounds, 217 MiB** against 100, and **the map at 3x, 500 MiB** against the 450 accepted. Known, unchanged.
- **Warm 2 GiB open, 273 ms** against 250. It was 198 in the last report at a different load; 281 p50 over 21 more starts here, so it's over at this load, not one run.

## Not tested

Frames reaching a display and 120 Hz; a visible window's memory; a true cold start (purging the file cache needs root, so the cold figures are a fresh clone's); Launch Services and Gatekeeper in an update's launch; background helpers' QoS; the off-screen byte budget of 256 MiB under pressure; NASA's picture pages in a sweep (the sweeps use `--file`).
